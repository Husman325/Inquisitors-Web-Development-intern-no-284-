Who We Are

INQUISITORS Society is an innovative student-driven organization focused on empowering students with industry-relevant technical knowledge, AI technologies, research capabilities, leadership, communication skills, and professional development.

We believe learning should extend beyond classrooms. Our goal is to connect students with real-world projects, industry mentors, cutting-edge AI tools, and collaborative environments that prepare them for future careers.

Our Mission

To inspire students to become future innovators by providing quality education, research opportunities, industry exposure, leadership experiences, and hands-on practical learning in Artificial Intelligence, Web Technologies, Research, Automation, and Emerging Technologies.

Our Vision

To become one of the leading student communities that prepares future professionals capable of solving real-world problems through innovation, technology, teamwork, ethical leadership, and continuous learning.
