About INQUISITORS Society

INQUISITORS Society is a student-led innovation and technology community committed to developing future leaders through Artificial Intelligence, Research, Technology, Innovation, and Professional Development.

The society provides practical learning experiences, professional mentorship, technical workshops, research opportunities, and industry-oriented projects to help students become future-ready professionals.

Our Story

INQUISITORS Society was founded with the vision of creating a learning environment where students can explore emerging technologies while improving their technical, professional, and leadership skills.

The organization encourages curiosity, problem-solving, innovation, teamwork, and lifelong learning.

Mission

To empower students through education, innovation, research, leadership, professional mentorship, and hands-on learning experiences.

Vision

To become one of the leading student technology communities that inspires innovation, supports research, and prepares students for successful careers.
